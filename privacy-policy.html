Rewatched

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Rewatched collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and how you can access, export, or delete it. Rewatched is developed and operated by an individual developer, not a company.

What information we collect

Rewatched collects only what's needed to run the app's tracking, social and subscription features, and to show ads on free accounts:

CategoryExamplesWhy we collect it
Account info Email address, display name/username Sign-in and identifying you to other users (your username is shown publicly next to comments and votes)
Profile info you add Avatar image, bio text, a header image you pick Shown on your public profile, entirely optional
Watch activity Shows you track, episodes you mark watched, favorites The core tracking feature, and to show your activity/stats on your profile
Social activity Episode/show comments, comment upvotes, emotion votes, favorite-character votes, star ratings, pace and complexity votes on shows, who you follow The social features described in the app
Lists and notes Show and episode lists you create; private notes you write on episodes Lists can be public or private, as you choose. Notes are private: only you can read them
Subscription status Whether your account has Premium, the Google Play purchase it comes from (a purchase token, the plan, its state and renewal date) To give Premium to the account that bought it, and to end it when the subscription ends or is refunded
Imported watch history Shows and watched episodes from a TVTime data export you choose to upload Only if you use the "Import from TVTime" feature — the file is read on your device and only the resulting show/episode matches are sent to our backend, never the raw export file itself
Diagnostic data Crash reports, app performance data, device model/OS version Finding and fixing bugs (see "Crash reporting & analytics" below)
Advertising data (free accounts only) Your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads — collected by Google AdMob, not by us Showing ads on free accounts (see "Ads" below). Premium accounts never load the ads software at all

We never see or store your payment details. Premium is bought and billed entirely through Google Play; we only receive the purchase itself, which we confirm with Google and record against your account.

How your information is used

  • To operate your account, your watchlist, and your watch history across devices.
  • To show your public profile, comments, votes, and ratings to other users, as intended by the app's social features.
  • To send you in-app notifications you've enabled (e.g. "an episode is airing soon") — these are generated and shown entirely on your own device, not through a push-notification or email service.
  • To identify and fix bugs and crashes.
  • To give Premium to the account that bought it, and to enforce the free tier's limits (3 lists, 20 notes) on accounts without it.
  • To show ads on free accounts.

We do not sell your personal information. We don't build advertising profiles ourselves and never pass your account information, watch history, lists, notes or social activity to advertisers. Ads are chosen by Google AdMob, as described under "Ads" below.

We don't share your personal information with anyone else, except where required by law.

Crash reporting & analytics

Rewatched uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports and error logs, and Firebase Analytics for basic, aggregate usage data (e.g. which screens are used). Crash reports are tagged with your account's internal user ID so that account-specific bugs can be investigated, but do not include your email, comments, or watch history. This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy, linked above.

Ads

Free accounts see a few ads inside the app, served by Google AdMob. Premium accounts see none, and on a Premium account the ads software isn't even started.

AdMob uses your device's advertising ID and approximate location to choose and measure ads. If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent form; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads, depending on the device). Ads are limited to those suitable for teenagers. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your data, your control

Download your data. Settings → Download my data exports a JSON file containing every row associated with your account — your profile, tracked shows, watched episodes, comments, votes, ratings, follows, lists, notes and subscription records — directly to a file on your device. Nothing is emailed or held anywhere else; you choose where the file is saved.

Delete your account. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account. This is not a soft delete: your account row is removed from our authentication system, which cascades through our database and permanently removes every piece of data tied to your account — profile, avatar, watch history, comments, votes, ratings, follows, lists, notes and subscription records. This action cannot be undone. If you no longer have the app, you can ask for your account to be deleted on this page.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription. Google Play bills your Google account, which isn't affected by deleting your Rewatched account. Cancel the subscription in Google Play first (Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can access, download, correct and delete your data using the app's own tools described above, or by writing to the address below. If you are in the European Union or the UK, you also have the right to restrict or object to how your data is used, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

We use your data to provide the service you signed up for (your account, tracking, social features and Premium), because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the app working (crash reports and basic analytics), and, for personalised ads in the EEA and UK, on the basis of the consent you give in the consent form. Our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their own privacy policies.

Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, the data described above is deleted immediately and permanently, as described above. Google keeps its own records of Play purchases, under its own policies. Backups of our database (if any exist at the time) age out and are overwritten in the ordinary course of our backend provider's retention schedule.

Children's privacy

Rewatched is meant for people aged 16 and over, and isn't directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
