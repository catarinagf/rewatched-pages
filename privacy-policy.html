Rewatched

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Rewatched collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and how you can access, export, or delete it. Rewatched is developed and operated by an individual developer, not a company.

What information we collect

Rewatched collects only what's needed to run the app's tracking and social features:

CategoryExamplesWhy we collect it
Account info Email address, display name/username Sign-in and identifying you to other users (your username is shown publicly next to comments and votes)
Profile info you add Avatar image, bio text, a header image you pick Shown on your public profile, entirely optional
Watch activity Shows you track, episodes you mark watched, favorites The core tracking feature, and to show your activity/stats on your profile
Social activity Episode/show comments, comment upvotes, emotion votes, favorite-character votes, star ratings, who you follow The social features described in the app
Imported watch history Shows and watched episodes from a TVTime data export you choose to upload Only if you use the "Import from TVTime" feature — the file is read on your device and only the resulting show/episode matches are sent to our backend, never the raw export file itself
Diagnostic data Crash reports, app performance data, device model/OS version Finding and fixing bugs (see "Crash reporting & analytics" below)

We do not collect payment information directly — Rewatched's Premium tier (when enabled) is billed entirely through Google Play's own billing system, which has its own privacy policy.

How your information is used

  • To operate your account, your watchlist, and your watch history across devices.
  • To show your public profile, comments, votes, and ratings to other users, as intended by the app's social features.
  • To send you in-app notifications you've enabled (e.g. "an episode is airing soon") — these are generated and shown entirely on your own device, not through a push-notification or email service.
  • To identify and fix bugs and crashes.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising profiles.

Who we share information with

Rewatched is built on a small number of third-party services, each handling a specific job:

ServiceWhat it handles
Supabase Our backend: account authentication, and storage of your profile, watch history, and social activity described above.
Google Sign-In An optional sign-in method, if you choose to sign in with your Google account instead of email/password.
Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics (Google) Crash and error reporting, and basic app-usage analytics — see below.
TVMaze Public show/episode metadata (titles, air dates, images) — no personal account information is sent to TVMaze.

We don't share your personal information with anyone else, except where required by law.

Crash reporting & analytics

Rewatched uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports and error logs, and Firebase Analytics for basic, aggregate usage data (e.g. which screens are used). Crash reports are tagged with your account's internal user ID so that account-specific bugs can be investigated, but do not include your email, comments, or watch history. This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy, linked above.

Your data, your control

Download your data. Settings → Download my data exports a JSON file containing every row associated with your account — your profile, tracked shows, watched episodes, comments, votes, ratings, and follows — directly to a file on your device. Nothing is emailed or held anywhere else; you choose where the file is saved.

Delete your account. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account. This is not a soft delete: your account row is removed from our authentication system, which cascades through our database and permanently removes every piece of data tied to your account — profile, avatar, watch history, comments, votes, ratings, and follows. This action cannot be undone.

Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, the data described above is deleted immediately and permanently, as described above. Backups of our database (if any exist at the time) age out and are overwritten in the ordinary course of our backend provider's retention schedule.

Children's privacy

Rewatched is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
